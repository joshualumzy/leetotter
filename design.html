Leetotter

A tutor for the NeetCode 150 that practises like an interview.

An AI in the NeetCode sidebar already helps, but it forgets me. It doesn't know what it taught me, what to give me next, or whether I'm retaining anything. Leetotter adds that memory and planning, with as little friction as possible, so I can solve the 150 on my own and stay ready. It is a Chrome extension with a small app on my Mac.

Design principles

Stay out of the way

No hint until I ask, and no interruptions while I work.

Cap the time

Every problem has a quiet countdown, one extension and a debrief, so none swallows an hour.

Choose the order for me

Topics are shuffled, misses return and patterns stay hidden until solved. Now and then it goes past the 150.

Let me steer

"More DP" or "fewer arrays" changes what it suggests and nothing else.

Be a little fun

Some personality, never distracting.
